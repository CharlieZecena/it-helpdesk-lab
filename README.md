# IT Helpdesk Lab — Jira Service Management

This project documents my practice managing IT support tickets in Jira Service Management, from the initial request through investigation, customer communication, and resolution.

I created this lab to develop practical helpdesk skills and demonstrate how I approach troubleshooting and document my work.

## Skills Practiced

- Creating, assigning, and updating support tickets
- Asking questions to identify symptoms and determine who is affected
- Interpreting simulated network troubleshooting results
- Writing internal technical notes and customer-facing replies
- Recording verification results and obtaining customer confirmation before resolving a ticket

## Case 1: Connected to Wi-Fi but Unable to Browse Websites

A fictional employee reported that their laptop connected to office Wi-Fi but could not load websites or connect to Microsoft Teams.

The scenario used simulated results from `ipconfig`, `ping`, and `nslookup` to identify an incorrect manually configured DNS server. The simulated fix restored automatic DNS settings, followed by successful DNS lookups, application checks, and customer confirmation.

The ticket was marked **Resolved**, with the resolution **Fixed**.

## Lab Scope

The ticket workflow was performed in Jira Service Management. The Windows environment, diagnostic outputs, configuration changes, and customer responses were simulated for training.

For the first case, I used one account to act as both the customer and the support agent.
